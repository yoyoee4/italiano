### Exercise Types by Section (Reference)

**Ascolto (Listening)**
- Multiple choice (single answer)
- True/False/Not Given
- Matching (speaker → statement)
- Gap fill (short answers)

**Lettura (Reading)**
- Multiple choice (single answer)
- True/False/Not Given
- Matching (heading → paragraph)
- Gap fill (grammar/vocabulary)
- Short answer questions

**Scrittura (Writing)**
- Short message/email (50-100 words)
- Formal letter/essay (150-250 words)
- Report/Article (B2+)

**Parlato (Speaking)**
- Self-introduction
- Picture description
- Role play (transactional)
- Discussion/Opinion (B1+)
- Presentation (B2+)

## Curriculum Progression Principles (Derived)

### 1. **Frequency-First Vocabulary**
- Teach top 1,000 words cover ~85% of daily usage
- Top 2,000 words cover ~95%
- Align with CEFR frequency bands

### 2. **Grammar Spiral**
- Introduce → Practice → Recycle → Expand
- A1: Presente, articoli, aggettivi, preposizioni semplici
- A2: Passato prossimo, imperfetto, futuro semplice, condizionale
- B1: Congiuntivo presente, periodo ipotetico (1° tipo), pronomi combinati
- B2: Congiuntivo passato/imperfetto, periodo ipotetico (2°/3° tipo), forma passiva
- C1: Discorso indiretto, gerundio, participio, stile formale
- C2: Sfumature lessicali, registri, varietà regionali

### 3. **Skill Integration**
- Every lesson: Vocabulary + Grammar + Listening + Speaking + Reading + Writing
- No isolated drills — always contextualized

### 4. **Exam Alignment from Day 1**
- A1 lessons → CILS A1 practice items
- B1 lessons → CILS B1 practice items
- Exam Center recommends lessons based on weak areas
